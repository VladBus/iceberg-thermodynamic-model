## 1. Executive Summary

Stage 8.1 performed a rigorous forensic investigation of the post-initialization energy evolution and residual instability in the realistic EN4-initialized Arctic/Barents Sea ocean state.

**Key Finding:** The Stage 8.0 "stable 3-day run" was a **zombie state** — the 3D baroclinic velocity field collapses to NaN in 52.3% of wet cells within the first model day, while the 2D barotropic (shallow water) mode continues running independently, generating kinetic energy (KE ~ 1.3–2.1×10¹⁸ J/m²) that masks the 3D collapse.

**Root Cause Identified:** The thermal-wind initialization does **not** correctly balance the EN4 density field. The initial velocity shear is 100–1000× smaller than the thermal-wind shear implied by EN4 density gradients. When Block 200 (baroclinic pressure gradient) activates, it immediately accelerates the flow to ~13 m/s in the first baroclinic step, triggering NaN propagation through the Thomas solver (Block 210) and Block 280 correction.

**Classification:** **D** — Physical initialization imbalance identified. The numerical instability (NaN propagation) is a secondary consequence of the unresolved physical imbalance between initial velocity and density fields.

### 7.1 Thermal-Wind Initialization Error Analysis

The `thermal_wind_init.f90` computes:

```fortran
integral_x = Σ C8*Dz*ΔRO  where C8 = 0.25/dx
```

This yields `integral_x = 0.5 * ∫(∂ρ/∂x)dz` (factor 0.5 from 4-point stencil + C8).

Correct thermal-wind integral: `v(z) = v_ref + ∫ g/(f*ρ₀) * ∂ρ/∂x dz`

Model computes: `v(z) = v_ref - (g/(f*ρ₀)) * 0.5 * ∫(∂ρ/∂x)dz`

**Two errors:**

1. **Factor 0.5** (stencil + C8 combination)
2. **Sign** (minus instead of plus for V-component)

The Stage 7.9 report claimed the factor-of-2 error was fixed in Block 200, but **the thermal-wind INITIALIZATION module has a separate, independent factor-of-2 error**.
